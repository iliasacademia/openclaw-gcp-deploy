Connect Telegram

OpenClaw uses a Telegram bot to receive your messages and reply with AI-powered responses. You'll create a private bot in under a minute.

How to get your bot token

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather (blue tick ✓)
  2. Tap Start, then send the command /newbot
  3. Choose a display name — e.g. My Claw Agent
  4. Choose a username ending in bot — e.g. myclawagent_bot
  5. BotFather replies with your token — copy it (looks like 123456789:ABCdef…)

Connect Google

This lets OpenClaw read your Gmail, search your Drive, and manage your Calendar. Google requires three short setup steps in their console — we'll guide you through each.

  1. Configure the OAuth consent screen (one-time, ~30 seconds). Open in a new tab →
    The page that opens says "Google Auth Platform not configured yet" — click the blue Get started button to open the setup wizard. The wizard has four sections; fill each one exactly like this:
    1. App Information
      • App name: My OpenClaw (any name works)
      • User support email: pick your own Google account from the dropdown — the same one you're signed in as
      • Click Next
    2. Audience
      • Select External
      • Click Next
    3. Contact Information
      • Email addresses: your own Google account email (the same one as above — this is where Google contacts you about your OAuth app)
      • Click Next
    4. Finish
      • Tick the agreement checkbox
      • Click Continue
    One more step Google doesn't include in the wizard:
    • In the left sidebar of the Google Auth Platform page, click Audience
    • Scroll to the Test users section and click + Add users
    • Enter your own Google account email → click Save
    Without this, OAuth approval later will fail with an "access blocked" error.
  2. Create an OAuth client (~20 seconds). Open Clients page →
    • Click + Create client (the blue button at the top of the Clients page)
    • Application type: select Desktop app from the dropdown
    • Name: My OpenClaw Client (any name works)
    • Click Create
    • A dialog appears with the Client ID and Client Secret — click Download JSON and save the file to your computer
  3. Upload the JSON file
    Drop (or pick) the client_secret_*.json file you just downloaded from Google. We read it locally in your browser — nothing leaves your machine until you click Save.
